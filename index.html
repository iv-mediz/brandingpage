What IV Mediz offers

One platform for professionals, hospitals, ambulance partners, and people who need a ride to care.

Jobs for professionals

Healthcare workers can build a profile, apply to openings, and share experience, documents, and availability with hospitals.

Hospital hiring

Hospitals and facilities can post jobs, review applicants, and manage hiring from the partner tools in the app.

Ambulance booking

Users can request an ambulance, share a pickup location, and follow trip status. This is a booking service, not a replacement for emergency number 112.
