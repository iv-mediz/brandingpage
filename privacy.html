Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · App: iv mediz (Android package com.ivmedizapp.user)

This Privacy Policy explains how IV Mediz (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the IV Mediz mobile application and related websites or APIs. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

IV Mediz provides healthcare job listings, hospital hiring tools, and ambulance booking. For privacy questions, contact support@ivmediz.app.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account details: mobile number, OTP verification status, name, email, date of birth, gender, city/state/district.
  • Professional profile: qualification, registration number, specialisation, experience, skills, education, work history, and uploaded documents (for example resume, council certificate, Aadhaar, experience letter) and profile photo.
  • Job activity: jobs you post or apply to, application status, and messages needed to process hiring.
  • Ambulance bookings: pickup and drop location, booking status, trip history, and details needed to dispatch a vehicle.
  • Partner data: hospital or fleet details, contact person, vehicle information, and online/offline availability if you register as a partner.
  • Device and usage: app version, device identifiers used for push notifications, and basic logs needed to keep the service secure.

3. Permissions and why we ask

  • Phone number / SMS OTP: to create and sign in to your account.
  • Location: to set pickup for ambulance bookings, show nearby services, and help drivers reach you. You can deny location, but ambulance booking may not work fully.
  • Notifications: to send booking updates, job alerts, and (for drivers) incoming trip alerts, including high-priority alerts when a trip is assigned.
  • Storage / files / camera (when you upload): so you can add a profile photo or documents.
  • Internet: to connect to our servers.

4. How we use information

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Show jobs, process applications, and let hospitals review candidates.
  • Dispatch ambulances, share trip details with assigned operators, and update booking status.
  • Send service notifications and respond to support requests.
  • Improve reliability, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Who we share information with

We share only what is needed to run the service:

  • Hospitals and recruiters when you apply for a job (profile and application details).
  • Ambulance operators / drivers when you book a trip (name, phone, and location as required for the booking).
  • Service providers such as cloud hosting, maps, and push notification platforms (for example Firebase Cloud Messaging) that process data on our instructions.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect users and the public.

6. Retention

We keep account, booking, and application records for as long as your account is active and as needed for legal, dispute, or safety reasons. You may ask us to delete or correct data at support@ivmediz.app, subject to records we must retain.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted transport (where enabled), access controls, and limited staff access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children

IV Mediz is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices and rights

You may access or update profile information in the app, turn off non-essential notifications in device settings, and request deletion of your account by emailing support@ivmediz.app. Where Indian data protection law applies, you may also have rights to access, correction, and grievance redressal.

10. Location of processing

Servers and subprocessors may be in India or other countries. By using the app you understand that your information may be processed outside your state of residence, with safeguards appropriate to the service.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The revised date will appear at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
